Origins

Our flagship full-day immersion into the mechanical watch.

Duration 6+ hours
Price $1,195
Students Six
Location Old Town Hall, Wilton, CT

Origins is a full day spent with a real mechanical movement in your hands. Under close, personal instruction, you’ll work through the anatomy of the movement piece by piece — disassembling, examining, and reassembling it — building an understanding that no amount of reading or watching could give you.

With only six students in the room, the pace is set for genuine understanding, not spectacle. By the end of the day, you’ll leave with a real, earned sense of accomplishment — and a lasting appreciation for the craft behind every mechanical watch you’ll ever encounter again.

Origins is intentionally small and intentionally unhurried. It is the deepest experience NovaChrono offers.
