About

The founder, and the mission behind NovaChrono.

Joseph Helpern

NovaChrono was founded by Joseph Helpern, who brings nearly twenty years of experience in science education to the world of mechanical watches. That background — teaching complex, technical subjects in a way that’s accessible and genuinely engaging — sits at the center of everything NovaChrono does.

Alongside that foundation, Joseph has pursued direct, hands-on training in watch education, learning the mechanics, history, and craft of mechanical timepieces from the ground up so that NovaChrono’s courses are taught with real depth, not borrowed talking points.

The mission is simple: build a welcoming, independent, non-commercial home for mechanical watch appreciation in America. NovaChrono is not a watch retailer, and it is not a luxury sales event dressed up as education. It exists purely to teach — to give people the knowledge and confidence to appreciate mechanical watches on their own terms.
