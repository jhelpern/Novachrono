Discover

A two-hour evening introduction to mechanical watches.

Duration 2 hours
Price $295
Seats Six
Location Old Town Hall, Wilton, CT

Discover is an evening orientation to the mechanics, history, design, and language of mechanical watches. It’s built for people who are curious but have never known where to start — you won’t need any prior knowledge to feel at home.

Over two unhurried hours, you’ll come away understanding how a mechanical watch actually works, where the craft came from, how to read design and quality, and how to speak the language collectors use — all in a small, welcoming room with five other newcomers.

There is no pressure to buy anything and nothing is for sale. Discover is an education, not a sales event.
